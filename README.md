Concept:
A top-down game where the player has to plant bomb in a casino where there are guards and the player has to maintain his cover and plant the bombs as he complete the plantings a timer starts and he has to escape the building.
Core Loop:
Player moves
hides from guards
plant bombs 
targets planted
and escape from building 
building explodes
Win & Lose Conditions:
Plant the bombs and then escape the building before timer all while not getting detected

Lose: Guards detection

Controls: 
WASD for movement
LShift for sprint
E for planting bomb
